1. About this policy

This is the official privacy notice of GamiFrench, a gamified app for learning French as a foreign language. It covers the GamiFrench mobile app (Android and iOS), the GamiFrench web app, the game's backend service, and GamiFrench's separate teacher / administrator dashboard. It explains what we (the developers of GamiFrench) handle, why, and what you can do about it — including when you use GamiFrench as part of a class or school.

Who is responsible

GamiFrench is an independent, education-focused project (it began as university research into gamified language learning). The developer listed on the Google Play listing is the data controller for your account data and is the “we” in this policy. Data about learners is processed to run the classes you join; when your school or teacher sets up class accounts, they are responsible for informing you and for obtaining any consent their institution's rules require (see section 10).

The spirit of this policy

  • Collect only what a learning app genuinely needs — nothing “just in case”.
  • Store credentials the boring, safe way: hashed, short-lived and encrypted.
  • Never build advertising profiles, sell data, or quietly add trackers.
  • Write privacy information like a person, not like a photocopier.

Where local law gives you stronger rights than this page describes, the law wins — you always keep those stronger rights.

3. Speech & pronunciation practice

Some lessons include speaking exercises: you say a French phrase out loud, and GamiFrench grades your pronunciation. This is the only feature that touches your microphone. The permission is requested the first time you open a speaking exercise — if you decline, that one exercise skips, and everything else keeps working normally.

How a spoken answer is graded

  1. Online (best quality): the short audio clip you just recorded is sent to Groq, our speech-to-text provider, which transcribes it and returns the text to the app.
  2. On the operating system's built-in recognizer: your phone's own offline speech recognition transcribes the clip, entirely on your device.
  3. On an offline model (optional): you can explicitly download an offline transcription model (~140 MB) from Settings; after that, pronunciation grading can run with no network at all.
  4. Deferred: if nothing works right now, the clip is kept only in the app's private storage on your device and is automatically re-graded the next time the app syncs.

What leaves your device — and what doesn't

  • Sent to Groq: only the audio file of the phrase you just spoke. Your name, email, account ID and other identifiers are not part of the transcription request. Even the reference sentence is deliberately excluded (it would bias the transcript).
  • Never sent to Groq: any other data from your account, profile, or progress.
  • Never sent to our own servers: the audio itself. Our backend receives only the resulting score of the exercise. We do not build a library of anyone's voice.
  • Under Groq's terms for API customers, audio submitted for transcription is not used to train their models; you can read Groq's privacy policy for their side of the story.
Clip lifecycle: record → grade → remove. A deferred clip lives in the app's private folder until the next successful sync grades it and removes it; practice attempts that produce no transcript are never stored at all; and clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes every copy instantly.

Microphone control

You can revoke microphone access at any time in Android Settings → Apps → GamiFrench → Permissions (or iOS Settings → GamiFrench). The microphone is used only while you are actively recording a speaking exercise — never in the background, and never for any other screen.

6. On your device

GamiFrench keeps a few things locally so you can play and sync later. Each item lives in the app's own private sandbox, which other apps cannot read.

  • Session tokens — a short-lived access token plus a rotating refresh token, stored in hardware-backed secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain, via Expo SecureStore). Your password is never saved on the device.
  • A small offline cache — your latest progress and settings, used when you play without a connection and uploaded at the next sync.
  • Speaking clips awaiting grading — see section 3. (On the web version, session tokens live in the browser's localStorage; clearing the site's data removes them.)

8. How we protect it

  • Passwords are bcrypt-hashed; nobody — including us — can read yours back.
  • Sessions use short-lived signed access tokens (~15 minutes) plus a rotating refresh token that is stored hashed server-side; signing out, a password change or a role change revokes them immediately.
  • Transport: everything travels over HTTPS/TLS in production, with HSTS enforced, strict CORS (only our own frontends may call the API), and rate limits on sign-up/sign-in that slow brute-force attacks.
  • Least privilege: learners cannot reach teacher or admin APIs, and every privileged action lands in the immutable audit record.
  • Log hygiene: request bodies (passwords, tokens, answers) are never written to logs, and standard hardening headers ride along on every response.
  • On the device: session tokens sit in hardware-backed encrypted storage.

We follow industry-standard practice, but no internet-connected service can promise perfection. If you ever suspect your GamiFrench account has been compromised, contact us (section 14) — we will reset credentials and pull the audit trail to see what happened.

10. Children & schools

  • GamiFrench is a general-audience learning game for users 13 and older. Self-serve sign-up is not intended for children under 13, and the app contains nothing aimed at young children: no chat, no comments, no ads, no in-app purchases, no open web browsing.
  • Classrooms: schools and teachers may create accounts for younger learners as part of organised instruction. In that case the school decides how GamiFrench is used and is responsible for informing parents and obtaining whatever consent its own rules and local law require; we process those classroom accounts on the school's instructions together with this policy.
  • Parents and guardians: if you believe a child's data was provided without appropriate consent, contact us (section 14) with the account's email — we will verify and delete it promptly, no bureaucracy.
  • There is no way for strangers to reach your child through the app: no public chat, direct messages, comment walls, or learner-to-learner content.

11. Academic research

GamiFrench grew out of a university research project on gamified language learning, and the developer continues that research. Here is exactly how that interacts with your data:

  • Only de-identified, aggregated learning statistics are used — things like “learners who kept streaks improved by X%”.
  • Names, emails, usernames and other identifiers never appear in research datasets or publications.
  • Surveys are voluntary (section 4); free-text comments are read to improve the app and are never quoted publicly.
  • You can opt out of research uses at any time via section 14 — we will exclude your record from any aggregate that hasn't been published yet.

12. Where data is processed

Our hosting and speech providers are global cloud companies whose infrastructure is primarily located in the United States. If you use GamiFrench from another country, your data may be transferred to and processed in those places. Those transfers ride only over encrypted connections, under processor contracts and (where the GDPR applies) the standard contractual safeguards that European law provides for such transfers.
